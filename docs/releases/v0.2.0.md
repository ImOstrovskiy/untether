The first public build of Untether: turn on your Android phone's hotspot from the Mac menu bar over
Bluetooth, no root.

**Downloads**

- `Untether-0.2.0.apk`: the phone app, Android 12 or newer, needs [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/).
- `Untether-0.2.0.dmg`: the Mac menu bar app, macOS 14 or newer, Apple Silicon.

**What's inside**

- One click on the Mac: the phone starts its hotspot and the Mac joins it in about 4–5 s.
- Battery, temperature, operator, network type, RSRP/SINR with graphs, devices on the hotspot.
- Choose the data SIM, reconnect mobile data, block a device, ring the phone or the Mac.
- Idle auto-off, battery guard, off when the Mac sleeps or leaves the hotspot.
- Signed commands with one-time nonces over an encrypted, bonded BLE link.
- English and Ukrainian.

**First launch on the Mac.** The app is self-signed and not notarized: right-click → **Open**, or
run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Untether.app`.

**Play Protect.** It may block the APK as harmful: the app is installed from outside Google Play
and switches the hotspot through hidden system APIs (via Shizuku). Tap **Install anyway**. The APK is built by GitHub Actions straight from this repository.

**Known limits.** Tested on a Pixel 9 with Android 17; Android 12–16 builds are expected to work
but have not been tried on a device yet. Reports are welcome.
