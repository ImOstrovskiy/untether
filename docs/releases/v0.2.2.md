**The menu bar tells it all.** A grey mark while the phone is out of reach, the mark while the
hotspot is off, and once it is on, the phone's signal alone: iPhone-style bars with the network type
(5G, LTE, 3G, 2G, or Wi-Fi when the phone shares its own Wi-Fi). The mark blinks while the hotspot
goes on or off.

**New**

- Quick connect: a click on the menu bar icon turns the hotspot on or off; right-click (or
  Option-click) opens the window. Settings → Quick connect.
- Network mode for the mobile data SIM, from the Mac or the phone: Auto, 5G, LTE, 3G or 2G. 5G keeps
  LTE as the anchor that non-standalone 5G needs and is offered only where the SIM allows 5G; Auto
  gives back exactly what the phone had before.
- Signal for every generation: RSRP and SINR on 5G and LTE, RSCP and Ec/No on 3G, RSSI on 2G.
- Use Android's hotspot settings: keep the name, password and band set in Android instead of
  Untether's own.
- Stop Untether on the phone, from its notification or the app. It stays stopped, also after a
  reboot, until you start it again, and the Mac shows the phone gone at once.
- The Mac app has an icon, and Cmd+Q quits it from the window.

**Better**

- After the phone app updates, the Mac picks it up again in under a minute instead of up to three.
- The DMG is no longer signed: macOS rejected the self-signed image before it even mounted.
- Phone app sections fill the width.

**Updating**

- Phone: install `Untether-0.2.2.apk` over 0.2.1; pairing and settings stay.
- Mac: replace Untether in Applications with the one from `Untether-0.2.2.dmg`. It is self-signed,
  not notarized: on macOS 15 and newer open it once, then allow it in **System Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 a right-click → **Open** is enough).
- Update both: the Mac's new checks expect the phone's new 30 s heartbeat.

**Play Protect** may still block the APK as harmful. Its verdict is tied to the app's package on
Google's side, not to the code, and stays until Google reviews it. Tap **Install anyway**; the APK is
built by GitHub Actions straight from this repository.
